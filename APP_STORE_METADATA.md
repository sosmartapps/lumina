```
Lumina is a comprehensive care management app designed specifically for people with Alzheimer's disease and their caregivers. With large, accessible interfaces and intuitive navigation, Lumina simplifies daily care routines, medication management, and family coordination.

• Easy-to-use home screen with large action tiles for navigation, calls, reminders, and contacts

INTEGRATIONS:

• Bouncie vehicle tracking for additional safety monitoring

Lumina is built by caregivers, for caregivers. We understand the unique challenges of Alzheimer's care and have designed every feature with accessibility, reliability, and peace of mind in mind.
```

```
Version: 1.0.0

OVERVIEW:
Lumina is a specialized care management app for people with Alzheimer's disease and their caregivers. The app provides medication tracking, location monitoring, reminder management, and family coordination features.

DUAL-USER MODEL:
The app supports two distinct user types:

1. PATIENT MODE:
   - Anonymous login (no authentication required)
   - Large, accessible interface optimized for cognitive impairment
   - One-tap access to: navigation, emergency calls, reminders, contacts
   - Text-to-speech voice prompts for medication reminders

   - NO direct data entry by patient required

   - Email/phone authentication via Firebase Auth
```

## 12. Age Rating Justification

### Category: **4+ (United States)**

**Rationale:**
- **No violence, profanity, or adult content**: App is family-oriented and appropriate for all ages
- **Location data**: While the app uses location services, this is a safety feature for caregivers monitoring patients — not for tracking children
- **No external links**: App does not link to websites with inappropriate content
- **Medical content**: Information is educational and clinical in nature
- **Infrequent/mild language**: If any, only in user-generated bug reports
- **Third-party services**: Firebase, Google Maps, Bouncie are all widely available in family-safe apps

**Caregiver-to-User Relationship**: While designed for Alzheimer's patients (typically elderly), the app's large interface, simple navigation, and parental-style controls make it suitable for younger users with cognitive or developmental needs. This broadens accessibility rather than restricting age.

**Result**: Lumina qualifies for **4+ age rating** with no content restrictions.
