## 4. Partnership Proposal Summary

### What Lumina Offers Partners

**Core Value Proposition for Alzheimer's Association:**

Lumina addresses a critical gap in the Alzheimer's care ecosystem: **the absence of a single, purpose-built, free safety and coordination tool for caregivers.**

Current fragmentation forces families to use:
- Separate location tracking apps (Google Family Link, Life360)
- Text/messaging apps (WhatsApp, Slack)
- Calendar/scheduling apps (Google Calendar)
- Emergency alert tools (various proprietary solutions)
- No integrated solution designed specifically for Alzheimer's workflows

**Lumina fills this gap with:**

1. **Real-time Safety Monitoring**
   - Geofenced safe zones with instant alerts
   - GPS location tracking (background services)
   - Wandering detection and notifications
   - Photo/video capture for identification purposes

2. **Caregiver Communication**
   - Multi-caregiver messaging (all caregivers see same updates)
   - Direct calling integration
   - Audio and text-to-speech support (accessibility)
   - Timezone-aware scheduling

3. **Accessibility & Inclusivity**
   - Purpose-built for Alzheimer's workflows (not generic)
   - High-contrast UI for elderly users
   - Simple, intuitive interface
   - Free for all families (no paywall)

4. **Research-Friendly**
   - Data export for clinical studies
   - IRB-approved study protocols
   - Open to feature feedback from clinical teams
   - Willing to validate impact through peer-reviewed research

---

## 5. Key Talking Points

### For Phone Calls, Meetings, or Presentations

#### **Opening (30 seconds)**
"Hi [name], I'm Leon Herbert, founder of So Smart Apps LLC here in Tucson. We're building Lumina, a free mobile app designed specifically for Alzheimer's caregivers. Wandering is one of the most dangerous behaviors in Alzheimer's care — about 6 in 10 people with dementia will wander at some point — and caregivers today juggle fragmented tools instead of having one comprehensive solution. We want to change that."

---

#### **Problem Statement (1 minute)**
- 7 million Americans with Alzheimer's; 12 million unpaid caregivers
- Wandering is a critical safety issue: if not found within 24 hours, up to 50% of wanderers suffer serious injury or death
- Caregivers use 4–6 different apps/tools: location tracking, messaging, scheduling, emergency alerts, family photos, etc.
- No single, purpose-built, free solution designed for Alzheimer's workflows
- This fragmentation increases caregiver burden and delays response in emergencies

---

#### **Solution (1 minute)**
"Lumina brings everything together into one app:
- **Real-time safety:** Geofenced safe zones with instant alerts if someone leaves the zone
- **Location tracking:** GPS tracking (with background services) so caregivers can see where their loved one is
- **Communication:** Multi-caregiver messaging so the whole care team is on the same page
- **Accessibility:** Audio and text-to-speech support, high-contrast UI, simple interface designed for elderly users
- **Free:** We're committed to making this free for families. No paywalls."

---

#### **Why Partnership Matters (1 minute)**
"We're not trying to build this alone. We need partners who:
1. **Know the community.** You work with caregivers every day. You know what works and what doesn't.
2. **Can help us recruit real users.** We want to test Lumina with 20–50 caregiver-patient pairs through your support groups or training programs.
3. **Can give us feedback.** Clinical feedback from your team helps us build features caregivers actually need.
4. **Can help us validate impact.** If you're interested, we'd love to do a formal research study showing how Lumina improves outcomes.

And for you, partnership means:
- A free, high-quality tool to recommend to the families you serve
- Co-branding and public recognition
- Clinical input into a tool that directly supports your mission
- Potential research credibility if we move forward with validation"

---

#### **Value Proposition (Alzheimer's Association perspective)**
- **Mission alignment:** Lumina directly supports the Association's mission of enhancing care and support for all affected by Alzheimer's
- **Gap-filler:** Addresses a technology gap; no existing solution is purpose-built for Alzheimer's caregiving
- **Free model:** Aligns with access and equity goals; removes financial barriers for families
- **Innovation story:** Positions the Association as forward-thinking on technology adoption
- **Research opportunity:** Ability to demonstrate impact of technology-enabled caregiving (publishable research)
- **Member value:** Tangible, free resource to offer members and the broader caregiver community

---

#### **For Academic/Research Conversations (AAC, UA)**
"We believe Lumina can measurably improve caregiver outcomes — caregiver burden, time-to-response, incident rates — but we want to prove it with rigorous, peer-reviewed research rather than anecdotes.

The AAC's pilot grant program is ideal for this. A 12-month study in partnership with a UA or ASU faculty member could:
- Evaluate Lumina's impact on caregiver stress/burden (validated scales)
- Measure response times and incident reduction
- Assess usability and feature adoption
- Produce a peer-reviewed publication
- Generate real-world evidence that helps other organizations understand the value of technology-enabled caregiving

We're open to customizing the app based on study feedback and are committed to data transparency."

---

#### **Objection Handling**

**"We already have a technology platform / app that families use"**
- Lumina isn't trying to replace what you have; it's a complementary tool designed specifically for safety and caregiver coordination
- If your platform doesn't have geofencing, location tracking, or real-time alerts, Lumina fills that gap
- Families can use both tools if needed; Lumina's job is to be simple and specialized
- We're open to integration conversations if that makes sense

**"We don't have capacity to partner right now"**
- No problem — when would be a good time to reconnect? (Note it and follow up in 3 months)
- Even a small pilot with a single support group is valuable
- We're not asking for a huge lift — just a 20-minute demo slot and permission to recruit participants

**"How do you sustain a free app?"**
- Long-term, we're exploring enterprise partnerships (health systems, senior living communities, federal programs like CMS GUIDE Model)
- We may offer optional premium features down the line, but core safety features will always be free
- For now, we're focused on building a great product and proving impact; sustainability comes after

**"What about data privacy and HIPAA?"**
- We take privacy seriously. We're HIPAA-aware and can share our privacy policy
- Families own their data; we don't sell or share personal information with third parties
- For research partnerships, we'll work with institutional review boards to handle data appropriately

## Appendix: Sample Questions & Answers

### FAQ for Caregivers

**Q: Is Lumina really free?**
A: Yes. Our mission is to make safety tools accessible to every family, regardless of income. The app is free and will stay free.

**Q: What happens to my family's location data?**
A: Your data is private and secure. We don't sell or share personal information. You control who has access to location data (you decide which caregivers can see).

**Q: What if my loved one refuses to carry the phone?**
A: That's a real challenge. Lumina works best when the person with Alzheimer's will carry the phone. We're exploring other options (smartwatches, etc.) but that's the current reality.

**Q: Can Lumina prevent wandering?**
A: No tool prevents wandering, but Lumina makes it much safer. With geofencing, you get instant alerts if your loved one leaves a safe zone. This gives you time to respond quickly.

**Q: How is Lumina different from apps I already use (Google Family Link, Life360)?**
A: Those apps are generic. Lumina is built specifically for Alzheimer's caregiving. It includes features like audio/TTS support, simplified UI for elderly users, and multi-caregiver coordination designed for care teams.
