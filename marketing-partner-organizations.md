# Lumina — Marketing & Distribution Partner Organizations
*2026-07-13 — outreach target list. Ordered roughly by effort-to-payoff.*

## 1. Hardware / device partners (already integrated = easiest wins)
- **Bouncie** — Lumina already links per-family. Pitch co-marketing: "the caregiver app for your Bouncie device." Ask for app-directory/partner-page listing.
- **SensorPush** — same play once environment monitoring ships; request listing in their integrations page.
- **Google Nest / Device Access partners** — Nest SDM integration qualifies Lumina for Google's Device Access partner directory.
- **Quad Lock / phone-mount & wearable accessory brands** — bundle angle for caregivers driving to check on patients.
- **AngelSense, Jiobit, Medical Guardian, Bay Alarm Medical** — wearable GPS/PERS companies; their customers are exactly Lumina's users. Integration or affiliate cross-promo.
- **Apple** — AirTag/FindMy ecosystem stories; also App Store editorial pitch (accessibility + caregiving category features).

## 2. Insurance (the "outside the box" money channel)
- **Medicare Advantage / D-SNP plans** — caregiver-support supplemental benefits jumped 5%→16% of SNPs in 2026; plans are actively buying caregiver tech. AZ targets: UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Banner–University Health Plans, Mercy Care (AHCCCS).
- **Long-term care insurers' wellness programs** — e.g., Assured Allies ("AgeAssured" for CalPERS), Nationwide LTC, Mutual of Omaha. They pay for tech that delays claims/facility placement.
- **AHCCCS / ALTCS (Arizona Medicaid LTC)** — managed-care contractors (Mercy Care, UnitedHealthcare Community Plan, Banner) fund home-and-community-based supports.
- **Life insurers with wellness riders** — John Hancock Vitality model; caregiving apps fit "aging well" programs.
- **Homeowners insurers** — some discount for monitored safety devices; long-shot but a PR angle ("home safety for memory-care households").
- **CMS Medicare App Library / Health Tech Ecosystem** — apply for listing once app shares data with caregivers/providers; federal-directory credibility.

## 3. Dementia / caregiving nonprofits
- **Alzheimer's Association** — Community Resource Finder listing (free, with AARP), local Desert Southwest Chapter events, Walk to End Alzheimer's sponsorship.
- **Alzheimer's Foundation of America** — grants sheriffs' offices money for wandering tech (funded Project Lifesaver expansions); natural fit for the LE-share feature.
- **Caregiver Action Network, Family Caregiver Alliance, National Alliance for Caregiving** — resource directories, newsletters, awareness months (November = National Family Caregivers Month).
- **Lewy Body Dementia Association, Parkinson's Foundation, Autism Society** (wandering overlaps) — niche directories.

## 4. Law enforcement & public safety (Leon's unfair advantage)
- **Project Lifesaver International** — the national LE wandering-response program; Lumina's LE-share screen is a software complement. Pitch as a partner tool, not a competitor.
- **IACP "Home Safe" program** — international chiefs' initiative on wandering; directory/pilot opportunity.
- **National Sheriffs' Association** — endorses Project Lifesaver; conference exhibit or magazine piece.
- **PCSD / Pima County pilot** — per county-comms style: frame as command-staff win, pilot small. A "Silver Alert prevention" pilot is a press-release machine for both sides.
- **AZ DPS Silver Alert program / state coordinators** — awareness-material co-branding.

## 5. Area Agencies on Aging & local (Tucson first)
- **Pima Council on Aging (PCOA)** — caregiver support groups, respite program, tech classes at The Katie; ask to demo at a class. (520) 790-7262.
- **Arizona AAA network (region agencies + DES Div. of Aging)** — statewide replication of whatever works with PCOA.
- **Eldercare Locator / USAging** — national AAA association; vendor listings, conference.
- **Pima County Public Library senior programs, Tucson Ward councils, faith communities** — free demo venues.

## 6. Healthcare & referral channels
- **Memory-care clinics & geriatricians** — Banner Alzheimer's Institute (Phoenix/Tucson), U of A Center on Aging: "prescribe" the app at diagnosis.
- **Home-care agencies** (Home Instead, Visiting Angels, Right at Home) — their aides + families are dual users; franchise-level partnerships.
- **A Place for Mom / Caring.com / Seniorly** — referral platforms with huge caregiver audiences; content/affiliate deals.
- **Hospital discharge planners & case-manager associations (CMSA)** — discharge-packet inclusion.
- **Pharmacies** (Walgreens — an AARP AgeTech member; med-sync programs align with medication reminders).

## 7. Accelerators, directories & credibility
- **AARP AgeTech Collaborative** — startup program; members include CVS Health Ventures, Walgreens. Best single door into aging-industry distribution.
- **Alzheimer's Association Community Resource Finder + AARP** — see §3; free listing.
- **findhelp.org / 211 Arizona** — social-services directories caseworkers actually search.
- **Employers/EAPs** — caregiver-benefit platforms (Cariloop, Wellthy, Grayce) that resell tools to employees; also large Tucson employers (Raytheon, UA, Pima County itself).
- **VA / veteran orgs** — VA Caregiver Support Program; Tucson VA is large, and vets skew into the dementia demographic.

## Suggested first three moves
1. Email Bouncie partnerships re: integration listing + co-marketing (cheapest, already built).
2. Apply to AARP AgeTech Collaborative + list Lumina in Community Resource Finder.
3. Ask PCOA for a 15-min demo slot at a caregiver support group (local proof → county pilot → insurer conversations).
